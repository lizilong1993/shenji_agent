结论：最小回归点应落在 [training/selfplay.py](/remote-home/lizilong/shenji_agent/training/selfplay.py:74) 的 `_load_case_data`，不要落在 `main`。现有 `_run_single` 测试已经把 `_load_case_data` patch 掉了（见 [tests/test_mainline_contract.py](/remote-home/lizilong/shenji_agent/tests/test_mainline_contract.py:605)），`main` 测试又把 `_run_single` patch 掉了（见 [tests/test_mainline_contract.py](/remote-home/lizilong/shenji_agent/tests/test_mainline_contract.py:723) 和 [tests/test_mainline_contract.py](/remote-home/lizilong/shenji_agent/tests/test_mainline_contract.py:892)），所以这两层都抓不到 old schema normalize/validate 回归。真实 `map_212/basic.json` 的 cell 只有 `nb/c/r/e/cd/rd/rv`，而消费者 [ai/agent.py](/remote-home/lizilong/shenji_agent/ai/agent.py:83) / [ai/agent.py](/remote-home/lizilong/shenji_agent/ai/agent.py:243) 直接取 `neighbors/roads/rivers/cond/elev`，loader 层是正确 choke point。
